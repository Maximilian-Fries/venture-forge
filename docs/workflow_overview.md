# Venture Forge 2.0: Workflow Overview

This document describes the current user journey through the application.

## 1. Stage 0: The Portfolio (The Hub)
**Route**: `/` (Root)
This is your "Mission Control".
-   **Sidebar**: Persistent navigation on the left.
    -   **Quick Switch**: Click any venture to jump to it.
    -   **Status Indicator**: Colored dots indicate state (Blue=Ideation, Amber=Debating, Green=Resolved).
    -   **CMD+K**: Press `Cmd+K` anywhere to open the "Jump to Venture" command palette.
-   **Dashboard**: Lists all ventures sorted by recent activity.

## 2. Stage 1: The Forge (Ideation)
**Route**: `/forge/:id` (or `/new`)
**Goal**: Turn a raw "shower thought" into a structural thesis.
-   **Split View Strategy**:
    -   **Left (Raw Input)**: You type unstructured notes (e.g., "AI lawyer for startups").
    -   **Right (Inflated Vision)**: The AI *real-time* expands your notes into Thielian concepts (e.g., "Regulatory Arbitrage", "10x Cost Advantage").
-   **Inflation Logic**: The AI looks for keywords and maps them to the 7 Lenses (e.g., "network effect" -> Moat).
-   **Exit**: Click **"Enter Gauntlet"** when you are ready to defend this thesis.

## 3. Stage 2: The Gauntlet (The 7-Step Crucible)
**Route**: `/gauntlet/:id`
**Goal**: Stress-test the idea against 7 specific risks. You cannot proceed until you resolve each lens.

### The 7 Lenses (Sequential)
1.  **Engineering**: Is it 10x better?
2.  **Timing**: Why now?
3.  **Monopoly**: What is the wedge?
4.  **People**: Founder-Market Fit (**Requires User Input**).
5.  **Distribution**: How do you sell it?
6.  **Durability**: Will it last 10 years?
7.  **Secrets**: What do you know that others don't?

### The "Loop" (For each lens)
1.  **Proposal**: The AI proposes a "Steel-manned" argument for this lens based on your Forge data.
2.  **Edit**: You refine the proposal. "No, my secret is X, not Y."
3.  **Debate**: You click "Enter Adversarial Debate".
    -   **The Visionary** defends the potential.
    -   **The Skeptic** attacks the flaws.
4.  **Resolve**: Once the debate settles, you click "Resolve Lens".
    -   A **K-Score** (Confidence Score) is calculated.

## 4. Stage 3: The War Room (System of Record)
**Route**: `/projects/:id`
**Goal**: The single source of truth for the venture.

-   **The Heatmap**: A 7-sector grid showing the status of each lens.
    -   **Green**: Resolved (High Confidence)
    -   **Amber**: Resolved (Low Confidence)
    -   **Red**: Dirty (Needs Re-debate)
-   **Dirty State Tracking**: If you go back to the Forge and change the idea, dependent lenses in the War Room turn **Red**. You must click them to re-run the Gauntlet for that specific point.
-   **The Dossier (Tabs)**:
    -   **Summary**: Your One-Sentence Thesis & High-level risks.
    -   **Competitors**: Editable matrix of threats.
    -   **Interview Lab**: "Mom Test" questions generated by the Skeptic.
