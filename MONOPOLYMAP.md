# MonopolyMap: Project Context & Manifesto

## 1. Vision & Core Philosophy
MonopolyMap is an adversarial venture architect designed to move entrepreneurs from "1 to N" (incremental progress) to "0 to 1" (vertical innovation). The tool acts as a ruthless filter for startup ideas, ensuring they meet the high bars set by Peter Thiel (Strategy) and Rob Fitzpatrick (Validation).

### The Foundation
- **Zero to One (Thiel):** We don't want to build "Uber for X." We seek monopolies built on proprietary technology, network effects, economies of scale, and branding.
- **The Mom Test (Fitzpatrick):** We never ask "Would you use this?" We only care about past behavior. We hunt for "Bad Data" (compliments, fluff, hypotheticals).

---

## 2. The Five Strategic Lenses
Every project in MonopolyMap must be evaluated through these five specific lenses. AI Agents must strictly adhere to these definitions:

| Lens | The AI's Critical Question | Success Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Engineering** | Is this a 10x breakthrough or 10% better? | Proprietary tech that is significantly better than substitutes. |
| **Timing** | Why now? What changed in the last 12-24 months? | Identification of a specific macro catalyst (Tech/Reg/Social). |
| **Monopoly** | Can you own 80% of a tiny, specific niche? | A "Wedge" strategy that avoids broad competition. |
| **Distribution** | How will you sell this without a massive ad budget? | Viral loops, complex sales, or built-in distribution moats. |
| **The Secret** | What important truth do very few people agree with? | A contrarian insight that others have missed. |

---

## 3. Adversarial Multi-Agent Architecture
The app does not use a single "helpful assistant." It uses a **Venture Jury** system:

1. **The Visionary Specialist:** - *Role:* Finds the 10x potential.
   - *Bias:* Optimistic, explores radical "what-if" scenarios.
2. **The Skeptic Specialist:** - *Role:* Finds the "1 to N" traps.
   - *Bias:* Ruthless, looks for hidden incumbents, physics constraints, and market apathy.
3. **The Investigator (Investigative Agent):** - *Role:* Applies "The Mom Test."
   - *Bias:* Skeptical of opinions; forces users to ground assumptions in past customer behavior.
4. **The General Partner (GP) Jury:** - *Role:* The Harmonizer.
   - *Task:* Reviews Specialist reports, highlights contradictions, and issues the final "Monopoly Score" and investment memo.

---

## 4. Technical Constraints & Logic
- **Project Manifest:** The "Single Source of Truth." A JSON state object representing the current state of the venture.
- **Threaded Context:** Each Lens is a conversation. The AI must remember the outcomes of previous lenses to inform the current one.
- **Scoring:** Not an average. A high score requires the **Skeptic** to be unable to find a structural flaw in the **Visionary's** 10x claim.

---

## 5. UI/UX Principles
- **Aesthetic:** "Venture Capital Dark Mode." Minimalist, high-density, professional (Linear/Vercel style).
- **The Debate UI:** Users should see the Visionary and Skeptic arguing in real-time.
- **The Wedge Visualizer:** Interactive SVG showing market entry vs. total addressable market.
